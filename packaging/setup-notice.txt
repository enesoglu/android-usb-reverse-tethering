USB Relay — Android USB Reverse Tethering

Free software under GNU GPL version 3 only. Copyright 2026 USB Relay contributors. No warranty. License and source links are included with the application.

This installer installs USB Relay for your Windows account. It does not install USB/network drivers or change Windows routing. The app includes its .NET runtime. Google platform tools are obtained separately through the setup assistant after terms consent, or supplied by you.

After installation, follow Setup help on Windows. On Android you must enable USB debugging, authorize your computer, and approve the VPN. Those OS security prompts cannot be automated.

Public alpha: sustained VPN throughput is still being improved. See the included installation and verification guides for measured results and limits.
